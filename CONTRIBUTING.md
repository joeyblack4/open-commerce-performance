# Help define portable business evidence

We are seeking source providers, businesses, consumer representatives and AI teams to review the [proposal](docs/CEO_BRIEF.md) and [candidate protocol](docs/PORTABLE_EVIDENCE_PROTOCOL.md).

For a source provider: which native measure could you responsibly issue with business permission today, what exactly would you check, and which records or outcomes would remain outside your coverage?

For an AI team: what issuer identity, method, population and lifecycle information would you require before using a claim during business discovery and selection? How would you explain selective publication and unknowns? How would you disclose commercial relationships and demonstrate that changing commission or ad spend does not improve your assessment of the same evidence?

For a business or consumer: which decision would the evidence improve, and which missing context would still change your choice? Which costs of a bad recommendation should a pilot measure, and what would keep smaller or newer businesses from participating?

[Open an issue](https://github.com/joeyblack4/open-commerce-performance/issues/new) with one concrete example. Please use fictional aggregates and do not upload customer records, credentials or private transaction data. The first interoperability target is consented claims from two source systems interpreted by two independent agents. No participation or endorsement has been agreed.
