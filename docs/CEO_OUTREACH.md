# Invitation to align on portable business evidence

Subject: Help businesses earn AI recommendations with evidence from their systems of record

Hi [Name],

I'm building an open proposal around a simple idea: **portable evidence about a business that an agent can consult during discovery and selection.**

Businesses choose what they want to publish. Their system of record, or an identified calculation service, attests to a defined result. Any participating AI can check the claim and use it when it matters to a consumer's decision.

A restaurant, an ecommerce brand and a B2B service company would publish different measures. They would share a format for explaining what was measured, the population and period, the source, who stands behind it and whether it is current. Customer and transaction records stay private.

I want good businesses to have a practical way to earn consideration through the service they deliver, and consumers to get recommendations grounded in relevant facts. Your team could help determine which claims your system could responsibly support today.

I've attached a short brief. Would you nominate a product and technical owner to review the proposal and scope a small interoperability pilot with source providers and AI platforms?
