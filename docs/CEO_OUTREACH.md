# Invitation to align on portable business evidence

Subject: Give AI recommendations evidence about what businesses actually deliver

Hi [Name],

Ad spend, affiliate commissions and sponsored endorsements can shape what consumers discover. The consumer still bears the cost when the business fails to deliver. I want businesses to have a practical way to make their service record count in AI recommendations.

I'm building an open proposal for **portable evidence about a business that an agent can consult during discovery and selection.**

Businesses choose what they want to publish. Their system of record, or an identified calculation service, attests to a defined result. Any participating AI can check the claim and use it when it matters to a consumer's decision.

A restaurant, an ecommerce brand and a B2B service company would publish different measures. They would share a format for explaining what was measured, the population and period, the source, who stands behind it and whether it is current. Customer and transaction records stay private.

Systems of record can make that evidence available. AI platforms must also keep paid placement and affiliate incentives separate from their assessment of what fits the buyer. A signed metric alone won't fix that incentive problem. Your team could help determine which claims your system could responsibly support today.

The [two-page proposal](https://joeyblack4.github.io/open-commerce-performance/proposal.pdf), [working fictional demo](https://joeyblack4.github.io/open-commerce-performance/) and [public repo](https://github.com/joeyblack4/open-commerce-performance) are live. Would you nominate a product and technical owner to review the proposal and scope a small interoperability pilot with source providers and AI platforms?

Joey
Black 4
