# Consented merchant pilot plan — local draft

No outreach, merchant access or publication authorized. Target 3–5 independently consenting distinct Shopify merchants after integration review.

## Hypothesis and baseline

Can optional scoped operational evidence help agents meet shopper delivery/price preferences without penalizing nonparticipants or misreading refunds? Freeze pre-period baseline, offers/prices/availability, service/geography, cohorts, policies, method, model/version/prompt before treatment. Compare identical candidates with/without evidence, plus equal-verbosity placebo and manipulated/missing arms. Randomize merchant order, blind independent raters and hold out outcome records where feasible.

Primary measures: constraint satisfaction, unsupported-claim/misinterpretation rate and consumer-specific regret against independently collected actual outcomes. Specify power, minimum practical effect and uncertainty method from baseline variance before seeing treatment. Synthetic regression results establish no real lift.

## Separate permissions

The exact business owner authorizes minimized source access, processing for named metrics and publication of the exact aggregate bundle/revision separately. OAuth is not publication consent. Explain unfavorable evidence, correction, withdrawal, expiry, caching and retention plainly. Use existing Platform Control identity/Reviews/receipts and Connector Hub permissions, never a new credential/authority store.

Verify current Shopify API/app/protected-data requirements and normal 60-day order access before justifying broader history. A mature cohort may be unavailable. Repeat purchase is deferred; do not collect customer linkage for it. Default orders cannot establish immutable checkout promises or independent delivery. Start with actual authorized evidence or publish unknown.

## Reconciliation and privacy

Reconcile source totals, all pages, duplicates, cancellation/partial/test records, history, missing fields, refund settlement and lag. Bind business/source/subject at every step. No names, email/hash, raw order/customer IDs, payment tokens or row digests in public claims. Review the bundle jointly for sparse diagnostic/complementary cells and cross-period differencing; k=30 alone is not a privacy guarantee.

Qualified review must settle processing terms, lawful access/publication, transfer, retention and deletion before real data. The handoff's proposed 30-day raw/13-month aggregate policy is not adopted here. Test disconnect, withdrawal, compromise, deletion and cached-expiry using existing platform owners.

## Correction, dispute and independent review

Merchants can dispute identity, completeness and calculation. Active material dispute suppresses definitive use. Freeze receipts/method; recompute; publish a reviewed new statement with supersession/status instead of silently rewriting signed bytes. Withdrawal stops new publication and revokes current claims. Cached bytes cannot physically be recalled, but status and expiry prevent current valid use.

An unaffiliated reviewer must challenge completeness, promises, carrier authenticity and gameability. Include no-refund policy, delayed refunds, subscription renewals, omitted cancellations, skewed geography, outages, sparse cohorts and verifier conflicts. Disclose funding; payment cannot purchase a favorable verdict. Actual audit requires a qualified independent auditor and scoped report, not OAuth/signature checks.

Only consented post-purchase outcomes and controlled comparison can establish consumer utility. Record negative/qualified results. Wider hosting, real access and upstream submission remain human decisions.
